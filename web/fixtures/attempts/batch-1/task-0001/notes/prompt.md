# Write notes on this attempt

Read the attempt's record and each visit's report, then write:

1. A verdict: `sound`, `flawed` or `unclear`, with your confidence.
2. What happened, in two or three sentences.
3. A table of each state, its visits and its result.
4. At most three lessons for the next attempt.

Write the notes to `out/notes.md` and the verdict to `tags.json`.
